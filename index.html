How it works

Up and running in three steps

Lumen collects your customer messages in one shared inbox. Your team claims, answers and closes them together, and you see how it's going at a glance.

  1. 1

    Connect your channels

    Link your support email, website chat and social accounts in a few clicks. No code needed.

  2. 2

    Invite your team

    Add teammates, set up assignment rules and share saved replies so everyone answers consistently.

  3. 3

    Reply and improve

    Resolve conversations faster, then use analytics to spot trends and coach your team.
